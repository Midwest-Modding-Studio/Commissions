How it works

Four steps from idea to delivered mod. Work starts only after you have approved a written scope and paid the deposit.

  1. Send a request

    Describe the mod, the features you want, and any references. Takes about ten minutes.

  2. Get a quote

    MMS reviews it and sends a written agreement listing every deliverable, the price and the timeline.

  3. Approve and pay the deposit

    You review the agreement and accept it online. Work starts once the deposit is received.

  4. Build and delivery

    Your mod is built to the agreed scope, with the included revision rounds, then delivered.

Clear scope, no surprises

Every commission is priced on a written list of deliverables. Here is how the rest works.

Only what is listed is included.Your agreement lists every deliverable. Anything not on that list is outside the commission.

Add-ons use a change order.Want something extra mid-project? You get a written change order with the price and any change to the delivery date. Nothing extra is started until you approve it. Minor additions start at $5.

Defects are fixed free.If a listed deliverable doesn't work as described, MMS fixes it at no charge.

Every project is quoted individually.Price depends on the size and complexity of the work. You see the full price before you agree to anything.

Deposit to start, balance before delivery.Exact amounts and payment terms are set out in your agreement.

Publishing rights stay with MMS.You can use your mod in your own game and on multiplayer servers you play on. Only MMS publishes it publicly on mod sites.
